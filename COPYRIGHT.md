# Copyright Reports

ARVIO is a media hub for user-configured services. Catalog metadata is not a viewing licence. Use only content and services you are authorized to access.

To report an asset, listing, link or other material controlled by ARVIO, email **arvio.app@gmail.com** with the subject **Copyright report - ARVIO**. Please include:

- The work you own or represent and your authority to report it.
- The exact ARVIO URL or app screen, app version, and enough detail to locate the material. A screenshot can help.
- A description of the claimed infringement and any relevant rights or permission documentation.
- Your name and a reply email so we can follow up.

Do not send account passwords, API keys or private playlist credentials. Reports are not public comments. We review reports, may request clarification, and remove or disable material or links we control where appropriate. An acknowledgment is not a determination of ownership or infringement.

We cannot delete files hosted by independent providers. You can also contact the relevant host, but you may still report an ARVIO-controlled asset or link to us. This contact process does not replace formal notices available under applicable law and does not claim registered DMCA-agent or safe-harbor status.

## Contributions And Demonstration Assets

Only submit assets with documented permission for the intended use, including commercial promotion where applicable. Do not assume an image returned by a metadata API is cleared for store screenshots. Keep provenance and attribution with each asset. Do not include private credentials, unauthorized media links or claims that ARVIO supplies a content subscription.
