A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Is ARVIO really free?

Yes. The Android and TV app is free and open source. You can also self-host ARVIO Web for free. Premium is the optional hosted web app membership that helps support the project.

Does ARVIO include movies or channels?

No. ARVIO organizes your own media services, libraries, addons and live TV sources. Bring the accounts and content you are authorized to use.

Can I use it on iPhone or iPad?

Use ARVIO Web in your browser. You can self-host it or choose Premium for hosting handled by ARVIO. Playback depends on your browser and connected source.

How can I support ARVIO?

Support development with a monthly coffee on Ko-fi, contribute on GitHub, or join the Discord community.
