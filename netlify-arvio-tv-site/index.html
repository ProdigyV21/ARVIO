Good to know.

Is ARVIO really free?

Yes. The Android and TV app is free and open source. You can also self-host ARVIO Web for free. Premium is hosted ARVIO Web for iPhone, iPad, Mac and PC: $2.99/month, or $20 for 12 months.

Does ARVIO include movies or channels?

No. ARVIO organizes your own media services, libraries, addons and live TV sources. Bring the accounts and content you are authorized to use.

Can I use it on iPhone or iPad?

Yes, with ARVIO Web in Safari. Sign in with your ARVIO account and add it to your Home Screen. Playback depends on your browser and connected source.

Read the iPhone and iPad guide

Does it work on PlayStation 5 or Xbox?

PlayStation 5 has no browser that can run ARVIO Web, so on a PS5 TV use the free ARVIO app on an Android TV, Google TV or Fire TV device instead. On Xbox, open ARVIO Web in Microsoft Edge; it switches to TV mode and works with the controller.

How can I support ARVIO?

Choose Premium if you want hosted ARVIO Web, contribute on GitHub, or join the Discord community.
