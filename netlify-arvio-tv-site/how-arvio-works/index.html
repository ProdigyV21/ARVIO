How ARVIO works, in plain words.

ARVIO is a free, open-source app that puts the things you already use in one tidy place: your media server, your live TV subscription, add-ons you trust, your watchlist and your music. It is the player and the remote, not the content. Nothing is included until you connect it.

The short version

Three steps, that is all

  1. 1

    Install ARVIO

    Free on Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV and Android phones. On iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows you use ARVIO Web in the browser.

  2. 2

    Connect what you have

    Your Jellyfin, Plex or Emby server, the live TV login from your provider, or an add-on from a service you are allowed to use.

  3. 3

    Watch everywhere

    Sign in to the free ARVIO Cloud account and your profiles, add-ons and progress follow you to every screen.

ARVIO does not come with films, channels or music.

Think of it like a music player or a web browser: very useful, but it only plays what you point it at. If someone tells you ARVIO “has everything for free”, they mean something they added themselves, and that is their responsibility.

The building blocks

Four things that make ARVIO work

Most questions disappear once you know these four words. They are separate on purpose, so one can work while another is missing.

Catalogs: what you see

Posters, rows, descriptions, cast and ratings. They come from TMDB, Trakt, MDBList, your server or an add-on. A catalog is a menu, not a kitchen: seeing a poster does not mean it can play.

Sources: what plays

The actual video or audio. A source is your media server, your live TV subscription or an add-on that returns playable streams you have rights to. When you press Play, ARVIO asks your sources and lists what they offer.

Trackers: what you watched

Optional. Trakt and Simkl remember your watchlist, history and where you stopped. They never provide video. Tracker guide.

ARVIO Cloud: your settings everywhere

Optional free account. It syncs profiles, add-ons, catalogs, live TV settings and progress between your TV, phone and ARVIO Web. Profiles and sync guide.

Stay on the right side

Where to get sources (the legal way)

ARVIO works best with sources that are yours or that you pay for. They are also the ones that keep working: no sudden shutdowns, no buffering at kick-off, no malware.

Your own media server

Put the films, series, home videos and music you own on a free Jellyfin server, or use Plex or Emby. ARVIO shows them with artwork and resumes where you left off. Connect a home server.

Your TV provider

Many TV and internet providers, and some broadcasters, give subscribers an M3U playlist, Xtream login or program guide (EPG). Ask your provider for the details and add them under Settings → TV. Live TV guide.

Free and official

Free-to-air broadcasters, public-domain film libraries, official channels from rights holders and, for music, artist-uploaded catalogs such as Audius. Plenty of good content is free and fully legal.

Add-ons from services you use

Add-ons are small web services that tell ARVIO about titles and streams. Only install add-ons from providers you trust and are authorized to use. How add-ons work.

A simple rule of thumb

If a playlist or add-on offers every new cinema release and thousands of premium channels for free or for a few euros, it is almost certainly not licensed. ARVIO cannot help with those, we will not recommend them, and they put you at risk of malware, blocked accounts and legal letters. When in doubt, leave it out.

Hands on

Your first 10 minutes

  1. Install the app

    On Android TV, Google TV or an Android phone: Google Play or the official APK. Fire TV: see the Fire TV guide. iPhone, iPad, Mac or Windows: ARVIO Web.

  2. Create your profile

    Choose a name and avatar. Everyone in the house can have their own profile with their own watchlist and progress. Children get a kids profile.

  3. Sign in to ARVIO Cloud (optional, recommended)

    Open Settings → Accounts → ARVIO Cloud. On a TV, scan the QR code with your phone instead of typing. From now on, what you set up once appears on your other devices.

  4. Connect one source

    Pick the easiest one you have: Settings → Home Server for Jellyfin, Plex or Emby, Settings → TV for your provider's live TV, or Settings → Addons for an add-on manifest URL.

  5. Play something you know is there

    Search for a title you are sure your source has and press Play. If it plays, you are set. If not, the playback checklist tells you why in a few minutes.

  6. Make it yours

    Pick an accent colour, reorder your home rows, hide tabs you never use and add collections. Optional: connect Trakt or Simkl.

Glossary

Words you will see

Add-on
A small web service that adds catalogs, subtitles or streams to ARVIO. You install it by pasting its address.
Manifest URL
The address of an add-on, usually ending in /manifest.json. Treat one with your login inside it like a password.
Catalog
A row of titles on your home screen or in a library. Shows what exists, not what can play.
Source / stream
The playable file or broadcast that a server, provider or add-on returns when you press Play.
Home server
Jellyfin, Plex or Emby running on your own computer or NAS, with your own media.
M3U, Xtream, Stalker
Three common ways TV providers deliver live channels to apps. Your provider tells you which one to use.
EPG
Electronic Program Guide: the “what is on now and next” schedule for live channels. ARVIO also uses it to find live sports.
Scrobbling
Automatically telling Trakt or Simkl what you just watched.
Debrid
A paid cloud download service some people connect through add-ons. ARVIO only plays what such a service returns; what you put in it is your responsibility.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Does ARVIO come with movies, series or channels?

No. ARVIO is software only. It does not host, sell or bundle any media. You see and play what you connect yourself: your own server, your provider's live TV, or add-ons from services you are allowed to use.

Is ARVIO free?

Yes. The Android, Android TV and Fire TV app is free and open source, with no ads and no paid tier. Only the hosted browser version, ARVIO Web, has an optional paid plan, and you can self-host ARVIO Web for free.

Why do I see posters but nothing plays?

Posters come from catalogs; video comes from sources. A title shows up because a catalog knows it exists, not because one of your sources has it. Connect a source that actually has the title, or check the playback checklist.

Is ARVIO legal?

Yes. ARVIO is a media player and organizer, like VLC or Kodi. Whether what you watch is legal depends on the sources you connect. Stick to your own media, services you pay for and official free content.

Can you recommend add-ons or playlists for free movies or channels?

We do not recommend or support unlicensed add-ons or playlists. Good legal options are your own Jellyfin, Plex or Emby server, the live TV login from your TV provider, free-to-air broadcasters and official free services.

Do I need an ARVIO account?

No. Everything works without one. A free ARVIO Cloud account is useful when you use more than one device, because it syncs profiles, add-ons, catalogs and progress.

Where do I get help?

Read the guide for your topic first, then ask in the ARVIO Discord with your device, ARVIO version and the exact message you see. Never post passwords or private URLs.

Still stuck?

Ask in the ARVIO Discord. Mention your device, the ARVIO version (shown under Settings → Accounts) and the exact message you see. Never share passwords, access tokens or private addon or playlist URLs.
