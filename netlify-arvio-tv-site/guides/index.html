Quick answers

The questions we hear most.

Does ARVIO come with films or channels?

No. ARVIO is a player and organizer. It plays what you connect: your own server, your TV provider's channels or add-ons from services you are allowed to use. Where to get sources

I see posters, but nothing plays.

Posters come from catalogs; video comes from sources. A poster means the title exists, not that one of your sources has it. Playback checklist

Is ARVIO free?

Yes. The Android, TV and Fire TV app is free and open source with no ads. Only the hosted ARVIO Web has an optional paid plan, and you can self-host it for free.

Will you recommend add-ons or playlists?

We do not recommend or support unlicensed add-ons or playlists. Your own media, the services you pay for and official free channels work best and keep working. Legal options

Software, not content

ARVIO does not host, provide, sell, bundle or redistribute media, channels, playlists or streams. You connect services, servers and files you are authorized to use. Availability, quality and playback depend on those sources and your device.
