The essentials.

ARVIO is a free, open-source media app for Android and Android TV, with a browser version. Bring your own sources, organise watchlists and home-server libraries, and browse live TV with an electronic programme guide.

Connect Jellyfin, Plex or Emby, and use Trakt or Simkl for supported lists and watch tracking.

The APK is free. Self-host ARVIO Web for free, or choose Premium managed hosting at $2.99/month to support hosting and development. Media and third-party subscriptions are not included. Browser playback depends on the source, codec, device and network.
