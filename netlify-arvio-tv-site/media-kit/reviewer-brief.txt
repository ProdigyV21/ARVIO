What it is
An independent media hub for Android TV, Google TV and Android mobile, with a browser companion. Connect your own Jellyfin, Plex or Emby server; add supported sources and organize discovery rows. ARVIO is not affiliated with those projects. No movies, channels, streaming accounts or third-party subscriptions are included.

Pricing
The Android app is free. The browser version can be self-hosted for free. Optional managed browser hosting is $2.99/month. Hosting is not an Android feature unlock and cannot guarantee that a source will work. Check the current Premium page for trial and membership terms: https://arvio.tv/premium/

A short demonstration you can record yourself
1. Install the official Android release on a supported device. Show Home navigation with a remote; do not use an included screenshot as proof of a test on your device.
2. In Settings > Home Server, connect a test account on your own Jellyfin server. Open Library > Homeserver, browse a library and test one film or episode you may legally play. Check audio, subtitles and resume.
3. If you run Plex or Emby as well, add a separately named connection. Explain that this is one interface for separate libraries, not a guaranteed merged/deduplicated database.
4. Open https://arvio.tv/collection-studio/#examples in a browser. Preview a starter, choose Make your own copy and rename a folder. Copy JSON and use ARVIO's collection importer. The Studio link itself is not an import URL.
5. Explain discovery versus playback: a collection lists metadata; the viewer supplies authorized sources. Browser codecs and networking differ from Android. Describe anything that fails on your tested setup.

Choose the part relevant to your audience; all five steps are not required for a short clip. This is a recording outline, not an existing video or a guarantee that setup takes a particular number of seconds.

Safety and editorial notes
Use your own test library. Hide passwords, configured addon URLs, server addresses, private lists and account details from recordings. Studio starters use only authored TMDB discovery queries; results are dynamic and not age-filtered. No paid placement, affiliate commission or required positive coverage is offered by this brief. Ask for permission before using anyone else's footage or artwork outside the context of the app screenshots.
